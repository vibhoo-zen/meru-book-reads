# Team Meru Reading List

A shared tracker for books the team recommends. Add a row when you recommend a
book, and mark the status as you go.

| Title | Author | Recommended by | Status |
|-------|--------|----------------|--------|
| The Pragmatic Programmer | David Thomas & Andrew Hunt | @vibhoo | To read |
| Designing Data-Intensive Applications | Martin Kleppmann | @vibhoo | To read |
| Refactoring | Martin Fowler | @vibhoo | To read |

## Status values
- `To read` — recommended, nobody has finished it yet
- `Reading` — someone is partway through
- `Read` — at least one person has finished it; add their notes below

## Finished a book?
Add a short paragraph under **Notes** with what you took away from it, so the
next reader knows what to expect.
